What we're researching

Mining is a technical business with real costs. These are the key areas to understand before purchasing equipment or offering a service.

⛏️

Hardware and hashrate

Compare the actual device model, hashrate, energy use, purchase price, warranty and expected useful life.

⚡

Power and cooling

Estimate electricity cost, ventilation, cooling, internet reliability and safe equipment installation.

📉

Profitability and risk

Mining income changes with network difficulty, coin prices, pool fees, downtime and operating costs. Profits are not guaranteed.
